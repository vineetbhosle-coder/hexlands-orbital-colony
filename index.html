Orbital Colony

Four colony ships have set down on the same mineral world. There is room for one capital. Claim sectors, lay lanes between your settlements, and grow them from outposts into arcologies before your rivals do.

The planet is divided into 19 sectors. Each producing sector yields one of six resources and carries a number from 2 to 12. When the dice show that number, every settlement on that sector's corners collects from it.

Ice and ore build your lanes. Helium, silicon and biomass feed your growth and buy chance cards. Antimatter goes into every settlement you raise, and twice into an arcology.

One sector is dead and yields nothing. The solar storm begins there, and whoever rolls a 7 decides where it goes next. A sector under the storm pays nobody.

You win by being the first colony to reach 11 points.

  1. Land. Click a point of light to place an outpost, then a lane beside it. Everyone places two. Your second outpost collects one resource from each sector it touches.
  2. Roll. On your turn, roll the dice. Sectors showing that number pay out to the settlements round them.
  3. Build. Spend resources on lanes, outposts, upgrades or a chance card. Hover any button to see what it does and what it costs. Short of something? Trade 4 of one resource to the bank for 1 of another.
  4. End your turn. The three rival colonies play; their rolls and moves appear in the middle of the screen.
  5. Watch the 7. Nothing is collected, anyone holding more than 7 resources loses half, and the roller moves the solar storm and takes a resource from a rival beside it.
  6. Read the map. Hover any settlement, lane or sector to see whose it is and what it yields. Left-drag turns the view, right-drag moves it, scroll zooms.

You have 15 lanes, 5 outposts, 4 habitats and 3 arcologies. Growing a settlement returns the smaller piece to your supply. The colony in last place takes one free resource at the start of each round.

You may play one chance card per turn, and not on the turn you bought it.
